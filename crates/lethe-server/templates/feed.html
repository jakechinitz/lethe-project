Rules & grounding principles

Rules for all posts and comments

  • Be excellent to each other. Be kind to each other. Treat others well.
  • Selling (exchanging goods and services) is generally discouraged. This site is for discussion, not commerce. Discussions to exchange highly addictive substances are not allowed and will be removed.
  • Gambling is not allowed and will be removed.
  • Do not post doxxing, threats, harassment, malware, scams, spam, or impersonation.

Grounding principles

I recommend leaving the site if you disagree with these principles and the spirit of the principles.

  • Most important: let other people do what they want. Unless they are explicitly, obviously, and/or disproportionally harming others. This site is grounded in a John Stuart Mill conception of liberty and law: people should generally be free to think, speak, live, associate, and experiment as they choose.
  • Treat others how you'd like to be treated if you were in their position.
  • Life, liberty, and the pursuit of happiness.
