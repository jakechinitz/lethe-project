Rules & grounding principles

Rules for all posts and comments

  • Be excellent to each other. Be kind. Treat others well.
  • This site is for discussion, not transactions. Commerce is generally discouraged. Do not use the site to buy, sell, broker, advertise, or coordinate exchanges of highly addictive substances. These posts will be removed.
  • Gambling is not allowed and will be removed.
  • Do not post doxxing, threats, targeted harassment, malware, scams, spam, or impersonation.
  • Insider trading and market manipulation: coordinated trading on material non-public information, pump-and-dump schemes, and coordinated price-action campaigns presented as organic activity. Posts soliciting, organizing, or facilitating such activity will be removed. Ordinary discussion of markets, analysis of public information, and individual investment decisions is not prohibited.

Grounding principles

If you disagree with these principles or their spirit, I recommend leaving the site.

  • Most important: let other people do what they want — unless they are explicitly, obviously, and seriously harming others, or doing so non-consensually.
  • This site is grounded in Mill's harm principle: people should generally be free to think, speak, live, associate, and experiment as they choose.
  • Treat others how you'd like to be treated if you were in their position.
  • Life, liberty, and the pursuit of happiness.
