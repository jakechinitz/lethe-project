Lethe

A Tor-native, anonymous public-to-private coordination platform. Public posts are public. Private rooms are end-to-end encrypted. There are no accounts, no profiles, and the server cannot read room messages.

You can prove continuity inside a thread without creating any permanent identity. This product cannot tell you whether a room or member is safe. It can show continuity, provenance, and the invite path.
