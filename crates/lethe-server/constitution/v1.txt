Lethe constitution v1
=====================

This text is the moderation floor every Lethe server in the federation
agrees to enforce. A server may locally enforce stricter rules. A server
that goes looser is defederated by its peers.

Versioning
----------
Bumping the constitution means a new file (v1.1.txt, v2.txt, ...) with
its own SHA-256 hash. Servers commit to a specific version on the wire
in their /api/federation/info response and in every federated event.

What is prohibited
------------------
1. Spam: duplicate-body floods, link-density spam, posts that exist only
   to drive traffic to an off-site location with no other content.

2. Malware and credential phishing: links whose destination is hosting
   malware, browser-resident exploits, or credential phishing. Origin
   servers are expected to take down such content on report and issue
   global removals so peers can drop it.

3. Targeted hate speech and slurs against protected classes. The intent
   is to follow the John Stuart Mill grounding principle the project
   ships under: speech is harmful when it crosses into direct attack on
   a person or class, not when it is merely strong language. Profanity
   on its own is not prohibited.

4. Targeted harassment of an identifiable person, including incitement
   directed at that person, threats of violence, and coordinated
   pile-ons.

5. Doxxing: publishing personally-identifying information (legal name,
   home address, employer, phone, email, biometrics) of a non-public
   person without their consent.

6. Sexual content involving minors. No exceptions, no "ironic" variants.

7. Direct incitement to imminent unlawful violence against an
   identifiable target.

What is NOT prohibited at the federation floor
----------------------------------------------
- Strong language, profanity, anger, sarcasm, satire, criticism of
  public figures and institutions.
- Discussions of substances, gambling, sex work, suicide and self-harm
  (in the harm-reduction sense), and other gray-area topics. Individual
  operators may choose to be stricter; the federation floor does not
  require them to be.
- Anonymous publication. Lethe has no accounts, no user identities, no
  durable handles by design.

How enforcement works
---------------------
- A server's local moderation pipeline runs at write time and again at
  federation ingest. Stricter operators drop posts looser peers
  accepted; that is the enforcement mechanism for "stricter is allowed,
  looser is not."
- The origin server of a post is the only authority that can issue a
  global removal of that post. Peers verify the removal signature
  against the origin server's pubkey before applying it locally.
- Operators choose their peers manually. Defederation = removing or
  disabling a peer. Already-replicated content stays unless the
  operator separately removes it.

What this document is not
-------------------------
- Not a terms of service. There are no user accounts to terminate.
- Not a court. There is no appeal process. The ultimate remedy is
  defederation.
- Not author-controlled. Once a constitution version is hashed, the
  text is frozen. Changing the moderation floor means a new version
  with a new hash, and operators choosing whether to peer with servers
  that have moved.
